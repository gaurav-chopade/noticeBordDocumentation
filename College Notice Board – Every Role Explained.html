1. Pick a role

Click a role. The menu unlocks, the dashboard numbers count up, and you see what that person can and cannot do. Roles come from the Role enum, plus visitors who have no account.

      2. Who can do what

      3. All roles working together

      One story, start to finish. Watch the paper notes travel between people as a student joins, a teacher posts, the HOD approves, the Principal broadcasts and the admin audits.

      4. Try the locks

      Pick a person and an API call. The request goes through the same gates as the real server: no token gives 401, a wrong role gives 403, and the right role gets through.
